AiPBX delivers native Android and iOS apps for field teams, remote employees, and executives: zero cloud reliance, direct PBX peer-to-peer encryption, and system-level incoming call wake-up.

AI PBX

Açık kaynak, yüksek eşzamanlılıklı kurumsal IP PBX ve Birleşik İletişim Platformu. Kısıtlayıcı güvenlik duvarlarını aşan Port 443 ALPN mimarisi, Asterisk 22, WebRTC, Go WebSocket sohbet motoru ve yerel mobil istemciler.

Open-source, high-concurrency enterprise IP PBX and Unified Communications platform. Featuring Port 443 ALPN multiplexing for restrictive firewalls, Asterisk 22, WebRTC, Go WebSocket chat, and native mobile apps.
