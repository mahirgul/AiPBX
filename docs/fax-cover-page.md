# Fax Cover Page

AiPBX can put a cover page in front of faxes sent from the portal. There is one template for the whole organisation, and each user can keep their own under *Send Fax* and choose one when sending.

## Setting it up

Open **Fax → Fax Units** and find **Fax cover page**:

- **Cover page on sent faxes:** *Off*, *The sender decides*, or *On every fax*. *On every fax* cannot be turned off by the sender.
- **Organisation name** and **Confidentiality notice:** printed on every cover.
- **Print the logo:** uses the PNG or JPEG logo from the **Appearance** page.

**Preview as a fax** shows the page in black and white at fax resolution, as the far end receives it. A fax has no greys, so
a colour logo can come out badly: a simple dark logo prints best.

## Sending

On **Send New Fax**, enter who it is for, a subject and a message. The sender's name and number, the recipient's number, the
date and the page count are filled in. The page count includes the cover.

The cover becomes part of the fax: the archived PDF shows it, and resending a fax sends the same pages again.
