Turkish Asterisk core sounds — AiPBX (asterisk-core-sounds-tr)

Generated with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, voice tr-TR-Wavenet-C, as a
16 kHz 16-bit mono master (silence trimmed, peak -6 dBFS, 150 ms tail).
One voice for the whole set: the Asterisk core sounds (same names as
asterisk-core-sounds-en), voicemail and AiPBX's own prompts.

Asterisk has no Turkish say.c rules, so numbers, dates and voicemail
counts are assembled in English order; the texts below are worded so that
order still sounds natural in Turkish (e.g. vm-youhave "Posta kutunuzda").

Non-speech files (tones) come from the English set:
ascending-2tone beep beeperr descending-2tone confbridge-join
confbridge-leave tt-monkeys; silence/1..10 are generated.

Build: web/bin/generate_tr_sounds.php (masters) →
       scripts/build_tr_sounds.sh (formats + package)
